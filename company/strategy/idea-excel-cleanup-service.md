# Idea brief: Excel cleanup-and-report agent (first product)

Status: proposed, under review by board, market-researcher and cfo. Written 2026-11-27.

## The idea
A customer sends a messy spreadsheet (sales export, timesheet, inventory list, bank download).
Within 24 hours they receive back: a cleaned file, a ready report (totals, pivot, charts) and a
short plain-English summary of what the numbers say.

Phase 1 (days 1-30): sold as a done-for-you service on Fiverr / Upwork. The founder runs an
AI agent (instructions + checklist + report templates, run in Claude) to do the work.
Phase 2 (month 2+): the most-requested job is packaged as a $19-29 self-serve agent the buyer
runs in their own Claude or ChatGPT account (no model cost to us), sold on Gumroad from YouTube.

## Founder constraints (stated by the founder)
- Customers they can reach: Excel / office workers.
- Coding ability: none.
- First sale needed within 30 days.
- Budget before first sale: $20.

## Existing assets
- A faceless Excel tutorial YouTube channel, planned but not yet live (see `youtube/`).
  Series C "Spreadsheet to report" overlaps this service. It will not bring buyers in the first 30 days.

## Known risks (to test)
- Fiverr / Upwork are crowded with low-priced Excel sellers.
- Customer files may contain personal or confidential data.
- Buyers may use free ChatGPT / Copilot in Excel instead.
- Phase 2 self-serve agents are easy to copy.

## Alternatives considered
- Excel formula explainer/fixer agent (free substitutes), Excel-to-dashboard agent (later upsell),
  small-business bookkeeping-in-Excel agent (higher liability, needs trust).
