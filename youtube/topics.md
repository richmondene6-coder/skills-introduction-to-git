# Topic list — Excel tutorials (faceless)

Built on `youtube/channel.md`, using the user's go-ahead on my guesses. Assumptions, not confirmed by the user:
- Viewer: people who use Excel for work or study and are stuck on a specific task.
- Format: screen recording with the user's own voice.
- Length: 5–8 minutes, one video a week.

**All demand claims are unverified.** Before scripting any topic, type its key phrase into YouTube search and note the suggestions and what already ranks. Drop or narrow a topic if the top results already answer it well.

## Suggested order
Start with #1, #2 and #3: narrow, quick, and they test whether the "fix it" angle finds viewers. Then try one from each other series. After about 10 videos, review which series viewers watched to the end and double down on that.

## Before scripting any topic
- Confirm the Excel version your screen recording uses (Microsoft 365, Windows or Mac) and say so in the video.
- Use your own sample data, or data you have the right to use. Do not show real company or personal data on screen.
