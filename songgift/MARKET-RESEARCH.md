# Market research: why personalized song gifts

Research done October 2026 for a goal of $200,000 in revenue by December 15, 2026.

## The niche

**AI-made personalized holiday songs, sold as gifts.** The buyer shares names, memories and inside jokes and gets a finished song with vocals, ready to give.

## Why this niche

- **Proven demand at scale.** Songfinch (human musicians, about $200 per song) made about $5.5M in 2021 and about $36M in 2022, and projected about $75M for 2023. It has delivered over 150,000 songs.
- **AI is cheaper and faster than the leader.** Songfinch takes days and pays artists $100 of each $200 sale. AI delivers in minutes for cents, so you can charge $29–49.
- **The holidays multiply sales.** Songfinch's order volume roughly triples in peak holiday weeks.
- **AI music is booming.** AI music app revenue was up about 410% year over year.

The app figures are third-party estimates or founders' own claims, not audited numbers.

**Other competitors:** GiftSong.ai, SongWish, Tunedforyou ($19.99), Songly, Melodfy, and many Etsy sellers.

## How SongGift stands out

- **One emotional product for one moment:** "your family's Christmas song," not a general song generator.
- **A free lyric preview before payment** builds trust and lifts conversion.
- **A printable QR gift card** turns a digital file into something to put under the tree.
- **Reaction videos** (people hearing "their" song) are the marketing. TikTok and Reels are the main channels.

## The math to $200k

- About 5,000 orders at a $40 average, mostly between Nov 20 and Dec 15.
- At about $12–15 in ad spend per sale, that's $60,000–75,000 in ads, paid for as you go by the sales they bring.

Honest caveat: most AI-built apps earn under $1,000 a month. Hitting $200k depends on getting buyers to the site (videos, creators, ads), not on the code.
